Privacy Policy – My Fin

Last updated: September 15, 2026

1. Introduction

My Fin is a personal finance management application developed by Antonio Alencar Velozo ("the developer").

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the application.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

My Fin lets you register and organize personal financial information, including but not limited to:

  • financial transactions and recurrences;
  • accounts, banks and account types;
  • people, assets, cost centers and purposes (categories);
  • application settings.

This information is stored only on your device. It is not sent to the developer or to any server.

3. Data Collection

My Fin does not require account creation and does not collect personal data for marketing, advertising or resale purposes.

The only data that leaves your device is the purchase information described in section 6, and only because in-app purchases are available.

4. Data Sharing

Your financial records are never shared with third parties, sold, or used for advertising.

5. Backup and Export

The application provides backup, restore, export and sharing features. These operations are started only by you, and the generated files are saved or shared wherever you choose.

You are responsible for protecting and securely storing those files.

6. Free Plan and In-App Purchases

My Fin can be used for free within quantity limits (for example, a number of transactions per month). MyFin Pro, available as a monthly or yearly subscription or as a one-time lifetime purchase, removes those limits. The limits are checked on your device, using the records stored there.

Purchases are processed by Google Play. The developer does not receive or store your payment details, such as card numbers.

To validate purchases and keep track of your access to MyFin Pro, the application uses RevenueCat, a subscription management service provided by RevenueCat, Inc. RevenueCat receives:

  • a random, anonymous identifier generated by the app (not linked to your name, email or financial records);
  • purchase information provided by Google Play, such as the product purchased, transaction identifiers, dates, price, currency and store country;
  • basic technical information needed to deliver the service, such as app version, operating system and device model.

This information is used only to provide and restore your purchases and to produce aggregated sales reports for the developer. Your financial records are never sent to RevenueCat.

For more details, see the RevenueCat Privacy Policy and the Google Privacy Policy. Subscriptions can be managed or cancelled at any time in the Google Play subscriptions settings.

7. Other Third-Party Libraries

The application uses open-source libraries for its technical operation. Apart from the purchase processing described above, they do not send your data to third parties.

8. Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect the information handled by the application. However, no electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

The data stored on your device is deleted when you delete it in the app, clear the app data, or uninstall the application.

Purchase records are kept by Google Play and RevenueCat as required to provide and restore purchases and to meet legal and accounting obligations. You can request the deletion of the purchase information associated with your installation by contacting the developer (section 12).

10. Children's Privacy

The application is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 9.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page, with the date of the last update at the top.
